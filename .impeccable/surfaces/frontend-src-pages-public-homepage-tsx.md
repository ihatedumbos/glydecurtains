# Surface Brief: Home Page (redesign anchor for the full app)

Scope: full visual-world replacement, storefront + admin. Visitor mode: Persuade (this surface); Operate register applies when this world is carried into admin.

Audience: homeowner/consumer shoppers and trade/bulk hardware buyers (equal weight, per PRODUCT.md). Job: believe Glyde is a real, precise manufacturer worth buying from, then browse to a category, a store, or a bulk-contact path. Proof/content: real product catalogue, real store locations, real achievement metrics - no fabricated claims. Constraint: never fabricate store locations, review content, or metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The storefront and admin abandon generic e-commerce card-grid chrome for the drapery workshop's own cutting table - pattern paper, chalk grain-lines, craft tools - refusing the polished SaaS-card-grid every hardware storefront defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Pattern-paper cream ground (#f4ede1), chalk-graphite ink (#2c2c2c), leather-brown (#8a6d5a) and brass-copper (#b5895a) accents. Lora for display headlines (warm, characterful serif - swapped from the initially planned Fraunces after the detector flagged it as an overused AI-generated-UI face); a clean neutral grotesk (IBM Plex Sans, swapped from Instrument Sans for the same reason) for body/UI and admin density; Caveat used ONLY for hand-drawn chalk leader-line annotations on hardware callouts - never as general UI type. Flat, paper-textured surfaces; depth from grain and chalk marks, not drop-shadows. Soft-cut paper-edge corners on hero cards.

STORY: A visitor understands within 5 seconds that Glyde treats hardware like a craft, not a commodity - proven by chalk-annotated technical callouts on real products, not just soft fabric styling. They browse by category, see hardware precision made visible, then buy, find a store, or reach bulk/trade contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed pattern-paper hero. Large chalk-graphite Lora headline ("Precision hardware, cut to fit your window"). A hero product photograph of a runner/track system laid across the cutting table like fabric would be, with hand-drawn (Caveat) leader-line annotations calling out real hardware parts - the raise that keeps this workshop world honestly about hardware, not fabric. A brass-accent primary CTA styled like a fabric swing-tag. Category icons below rendered as chalk-stamped grain-line symbols.

FORM: Tailoring Pattern-Cutting Table (safer register, option safer-3), seed key c47b954c, chosen after two re-rolls (plain, then safer) over the assigned Railway Signal, Gear & Pulley Cutaway, and their challengers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
